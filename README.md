## 🎨 Apple-Inspired Design System

- **Clean White Canvas**: High-end minimalist white palette (`#ffffff`, `#f5f5f7`, `#fbfbfd`) with crisp typography and generous breathing space.
- **Apple Frosted Glass Navigation**: Sticky navbar with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and hairline border.
- **Apple Typography**: Strict SF Pro / Inter typographic hierarchy with dedicated support for Telugu fonts.
- **Apple Pill Controls & Chevrons**: Rounded-full primary blue buttons (`#0071e3`), dark graphite pills, and minimalist inline text links with chevrons (`›`).
- **Bento Grid Cards**: Rounded corners (28px radius), subtle borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), and soft drop shadows.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Dual Language Support (English & తెలుగు)**:
   - Apple-style segmented pill in the navbar toggles between English and Telugu instantly.

2. **Official Social Media Spotlight**:
   - Clean white showcase cards highlighting Instagram `@vegesnarohini.official` (838+ followers) and Facebook profile (3,246+ followers).

3. **Academic & AI Research Portfolio**:
   - Spotlights her role as Assistant Professor at **SRKR Engineering College, Bhimavaram**, and published IEEE papers.

4. **Strategic Vision: "Swachh & Swarna Bhimavaram"**:
   - 6 Civic Pillars structured in an Apple Bento Grid.

5. **Media Coverage & Video Hub**:
   - Apple TV+ style video cards with modal playback for **SumanTV Bhimavaram**, **CVR English**, and **AKN News Telugu**.

6. **Praja Vani (Citizen Grievance & Tracking Portal)**:
   - Apple Support / Genius Bar style grievance registration form across all 39 municipal wards of Bhimavaram.
   - Live status tracking with a 4-step progress stepper.
   - **Automated WhatsApp / SMS Notification**: Automatically dispatches tracking IDs directly to citizen mobile numbers upon submission.
   - Supported gateways: **UltraMsg**, **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**, **WATI**, and **Fast2SMS**. Configured via `config/whatsapp.php`.
   - Secure office management portal at `admin_login.php` leading to `admin_grievances.php`.
