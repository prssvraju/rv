Family Heritage

Affiliation SRKR Eng. College
Jurisdiction Bhimavaram, AP

Vegesna Rama Bangaru Rohini stands at the intersection of modern academic intellect and grassroots civic leadership. As an Assistant Professor at the prestigious SRKR Engineering College in Bhimavaram, she has mentored hundreds of aspiring engineers and contributed pioneering research in Artificial Intelligence, Vision Transformers, and Blockchain governance.

Inspired by the ethos of Deputy Chief Minister Pawan Kalyan and the Jana Sena Party alliance, she entered public life to champion clean, transparent, and responsive municipal administration. She carries forward the noble legacy of public welfare established by her father, senior leader and Kshatriya Corporation Chairman Vegesna Kanaka Raju Suri.

STRATEGIC ROADMAP

Vision: Swachh & Swarna Bhimavaram

6 Core Pillars for Transforming Bhimavaram into a Model Municipal Town

🌱

1. Clean & Green Bhimavaram

Door-to-door segregated waste collection, desilting of major drainage canals, mechanized cleaning, and plastic-free ward initiatives.

Municipal Sanitation Focus
🌊

2. Flood Mitigation & Underground Drainage

Modernizing urban runoff canals and upgrading underground sewer lines to put an end to monsoon waterlogging once and for all.

Urban Water Engineering
💧

3. 24/7 Safe Potable Water

Automated filtration plants, regular pipeline testing, and reliable piped drinking water supply to every household across all 39 wards.

Public Health Assurance
👩‍💼

4. Youth & Women Empowerment

Modern digital skill incubators, support for DWCRA women self-help groups, entrepreneurship loans, and competitive exam coaching centers.

Socio-Economic Development
⚖️

5. Transparent & Corruption-Free Governance

Digital citizen service counters, transparent municipal clearances, and strict accountability for public grievance resolution.

Zero Tolerance Policy
🛣️

6. World-Class Municipal Infrastructure

Pothole-free arterial roads, energy-efficient LED street lighting in every lane, green public parks, and upgraded municipal school facilities.

Town Modernization
